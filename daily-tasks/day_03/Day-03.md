
# Day 3: Booking Analytics

## Objective
To analyze hotel booking counts and cancellation rates.

## Tasks Completed
- Loaded the cleaned dataset.
- Counted bookings by hotel type.
- Calculated canceled bookings by hotel type.
- Compared cancellation rates.
- Created two Matplotlib charts.

## Results
- Total cleaned records: 87,396
- City Hotel bookings: 53,428
- Resort Hotel bookings: 33,968
- City Hotel cancellations: 16,049
- Resort Hotel cancellations: 7,976
- City Hotel cancellation rate: 30.04%
- Resort Hotel cancellation rate: 23.48%

## Key Findings
City Hotel has more bookings and a higher cancellation rate than Resort Hotel in the cleaned dataset.
