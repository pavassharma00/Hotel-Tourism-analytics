
# Day 9 - Final Analytics Dashboard

## Objective
To combine important hotel booking analytics into one dashboard.

## KPIs Included

1. Total bookings
2. Successful bookings
3. Canceled bookings
4. Cancellation rate
5. Estimated room revenue
6. Average ADR

## Dashboard Visualizations

1. Bookings by hotel
2. Booking status distribution
3. Monthly booking trends
4. Estimated room revenue by hotel

## Tools Used

- Python
- Pandas
- Matplotlib
